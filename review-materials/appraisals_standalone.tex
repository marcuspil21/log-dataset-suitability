\noindent
This document is the audit trail of the systematic literature review. It
records, for each reviewed source, the acquisition channel, a summary, the
arguments for and against its use, the usability verdict, and the datasets the
source evaluates on. The thesis reports the aggregate results of the review and
its appendix carries the quality-assessment scores, the acquisition channels and
the thematic evidence tables; this document carries the per-source detail behind
them.

\medskip\noindent
Cross-references to the thesis are given by name rather than by number, because
the two documents are numbered independently.
